# Kirinyaga Coffee Extension Notes (2026 main crop)

Source: cooperative extension office. Trust level: trusted.
Everything in this document is invented for the Kahawa Guard demo.

## Coffee berry disease

Spray copper oxychloride 50WP at up to 100 ml per 20 litres of water, starting at
the onset of the long rains and repeating every three weeks while conditions stay
wet. Do not exceed the stated dose. Buy only from a dealer on the licensed
register and keep the receipt for the cooperative's records.

## Leaf rust

Kahawa Guard Leaf Rust Mix at up to 60 ml per 20 litres. Alternate with sulphur
80WG at up to 80 ml per 20 litres to limit resistance.

## Buying inputs

A dealer must appear on the cooperative's licensed dealer register with status
"active" and a valid-until date in the future. Suspended and revoked licences are
not valid, whatever the dealer says. If a trader is not on the register, the
cooperative cannot reimburse the purchase.

## Payments

Payout account changes are made in person at the cooperative office, with ID.
Nobody from the cooperative will ever ask for your PIN over the phone or by SMS.
